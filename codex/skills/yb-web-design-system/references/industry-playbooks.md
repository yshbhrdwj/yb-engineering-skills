# Industry playbooks

Read one playbook, the one matching the product. These are context to reason from, not templates: the product's own audience and brand override them. Each lists goals; density; trust expectations; suitable directions; navigation norms; accessibility concerns; typical failures; and where differentiation comes from.

## Fintech and banking
Goals: confidence moving money, clarity of balances and fees. Density: medium; key figures large and precise. Trust: exact amounts with currency, clear status of every transaction, security cues that are real (not decorative padlocks), plain-language fees. Directions: restrained, high-contrast, generous whitespace around money. Navigation: accounts, payments, cards, activity. Accessibility: tabular numerals, never color-only gains or losses, time limits with warnings. Failures: hidden fees, ambiguous pending states, playful motion around money. Differentiation: clarity and tone of voice rather than ornament.

## Trading and investing
Goals: fast, accurate decisions under streaming data. Density: high, user-adjustable. Trust: data freshness timestamps, order confirmation summaries, unambiguous buy/sell separation. Directions: dark themes common for long sessions (offer light), monospace or tabular numerals, minimal decoration. Navigation: watchlists, charts, order entry, positions, persistent across views. Accessibility: change shown by sign and arrow plus color, color conventions per market (red-up in parts of East Asia), flash animations brief and reducible, keyboard order entry. Failures: layout shift under updates, gamified celebrations of trades, controls that move. Differentiation: information design and speed of the core loop.

## Healthcare
Goals: correct information for patients and clinicians, errors prevented. Density: low for patients, high for clinical tools. Trust: credentials, privacy statements, clear provenance of medical information. Directions: calm, legible, warm but not clinical-cold. Navigation: tasks first (book, results, messages, billing). Accessibility: older users, low vision, cognitive load, plain language at a low reading level, large targets. Failures: jargon, alarming color for normal results, dense forms without saving. Differentiation: plainness and reassurance.

## Wellness and fitness
Goals: motivation and habit. Density: low. Trust: honest claims, no shaming. Directions: warm, expressive photography or illustration, encouraging tone. Navigation: today, progress, library. Accessibility: motion-heavy content needs reduced-motion alternatives; avoid body-image pressure. Failures: streak guilt, dark patterns in subscriptions. Differentiation: voice and personal progress visualization.

## Enterprise SaaS
Goals: efficient repeated work across roles and permissions. Density: medium-high with a density setting. Trust: predictability, audit trails, clear permission states. Directions: neutral, systematic, strong type hierarchy, one accent. Navigation: sidebar of objects, global search, breadcrumbs. Accessibility: keyboard paths for power users, accessible data tables, long-session comfort. Failures: marketing composition inside the app, inconsistent components across modules, hidden permission errors. Differentiation: workflow speed and coherence, not visual flourish.

## AI products
Goals: users understand capability, limits, and provenance of outputs. Density: medium. Trust: visible sources, editable outputs, clear indication of generated content, undo, and a stop control during generation. Directions: avoid the default dark-purple gradient look; let the user's content dominate. Navigation: conversation or workspace, history, settings for data use. Accessibility: streaming text announced politely, not per token; stop and regenerate keyboard reachable. Failures: anthropomorphic overclaiming, no error states for wrong answers, spinners with no progress. Differentiation: how uncertainty, sources, and editing are designed.

## Developer tools
Goals: speed, precision, copyable truth. Density: high. Trust: accurate examples, versioned docs, honest status pages. Directions: monospace for code, strong syntax colors meeting contrast, keyboard-first. Navigation: docs sidebar with search, API reference, changelog; command palette in apps. Accessibility: code blocks keyboard-scrollable and copyable, color themes meeting contrast. Failures: marketing fluff in docs, unversioned examples, low-contrast code themes. Differentiation: information architecture and examples.

## E-commerce and retail
Goals: find, evaluate, and buy with confidence. Density: medium in listings, focused in checkout. Trust: real reviews, total price early, returns and delivery clarity. Directions: product imagery leads; type and color support the brand without competing. Navigation: categories, search with filters, cart always reachable. Accessibility: image alternatives, accessible filters and carousels (pause control), checkout without redundant entry. Failures: fake urgency, hidden shipping costs, carousels as the primary navigation. Differentiation: photography, merchandising, and checkout ease.

## Education
Goals: learning progress and comprehension. Density: low to medium. Trust: credible instructors, clear outcomes. Directions: friendly but readable; reading-optimized lesson pages. Navigation: course, module, lesson, progress. Accessibility: captions and transcripts, keyboard-operable exercises, younger and older learners, time limits adjustable. Failures: gamification over learning, dense video-only content. Differentiation: pedagogy expressed in layout (practice beside explanation).

## Travel and hospitality
Goals: inspire, then book without surprises. Density: imagery-rich browsing, precise booking. Trust: total price with taxes, cancellation terms, real photos. Directions: evocative imagery, place-specific identity. Navigation: search with dates and guests first. Accessibility: date pickers with keyboard and text entry, maps with list alternatives. Failures: drip pricing, fake scarcity, inaccessible maps. Differentiation: sense of place and booking clarity.

## Media and publishing
Goals: reading and returning. Density: editorial. Trust: bylines, dates, corrections, labelled sponsored content. Directions: strong typography, measure and rhythm first. Navigation: sections, topics, search. Accessibility: readable type, ads that do not shift text, video captions. Failures: layout shift from ads, interstitials, autoplay. Differentiation: typographic voice.

## Social and community
Goals: participation and safety. Density: medium feeds. Trust: clear identity, moderation and reporting, privacy controls. Directions: content-forward, brand in small details. Navigation: feed, notifications, messages, profile. Accessibility: alt text prompts for uploads, accessible infinite scroll (or pagination), reduced motion for media. Failures: engagement dark patterns, hard-to-find privacy settings. Differentiation: interaction texture and community tone.

## Gaming
Goals: excitement and smooth entry to play. Density: varies. Trust: transparent purchases and odds. Directions: expressive, art-led, motion-rich (with reduced-motion paths). Navigation: play first, store, profile. Accessibility: subtitle and control remapping information, color-blind modes, photosensitivity warnings. Failures: unreadable text over art, manipulative purchase flows. Differentiation: art direction.

## Productivity
Goals: focus and flow. Density: medium-high, calm. Trust: reliable saving and sync status. Directions: quiet chrome, content-first, keyboard shortcuts. Navigation: workspace, search, command palette. Accessibility: keyboard everything, focus management in editors. Failures: notification noise, decoration around content. Differentiation: speed and small, considered interactions.

## Portfolios and creative
Goals: show the work, show the person. Density: low. Trust: real projects, clear roles and outcomes. Directions: the most freedom for expressive type and layout; the work stays dominant. Navigation: minimal. Accessibility: image alternatives, readable case studies, experimental layouts still keyboard-navigable. Failures: style overwhelming work, slow heavy media. Differentiation: point of view.

## Professional services
Goals: credibility and inquiry. Density: low. Trust: named people, credentials, case results without inflated claims. Directions: confident type, restrained palette, quality photography of real people. Navigation: services, work, people, contact. Accessibility: contact forms and maps accessible. Failures: stock photography, generic corporate blue. Differentiation: voice and evidence.

## Real estate
Goals: find and evaluate properties. Density: listing grids plus detail. Trust: accurate price, fees, availability, real photos and floor plans. Directions: photography-led, clean data panels. Navigation: search with map and filters, saved searches. Accessibility: map plus list parity, accessible galleries. Failures: map-only search, outdated listings without dates. Differentiation: search and comparison tools.

## Logistics and operations
Goals: track, exception-handle, and act at scale. Density: high. Trust: timestamps, sources, and status precision. Directions: utilitarian, strong status system, tables and timelines. Navigation: shipments or orders, exceptions, fleet or locations, reports. Accessibility: status by text and icon, keyboard bulk actions, long sessions. Failures: map-centric dashboards hiding exceptions, inconsistent status vocabulary. Differentiation: exception workflows and clarity under volume.
