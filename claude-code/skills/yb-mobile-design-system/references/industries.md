# Industry playbooks

Decision aids, not templates. Read only the matching section; combine two when the product spans them (for example family plus health). Existing brand and research override these defaults.

Each entry: **Goals** · **Density** · **Trust** · **Navigation** · **Visual** · **Emotion** · **Failures** · **Access** · **Alternatives** · **Differentiate**.

## Health and wellness

**Goals** build habits, log quickly, see progress. **Density** low to medium. **Trust** privacy cues, gentle data use. **Navigation** today view plus history and insights tabs. **Visual** calm palettes, soft geometry, generous spacing, clear progress. **Emotion** encouraging, never shaming. **Failures** streak guilt, cluttered dashboards, hidden logging. **Access** large targets, plain language, reduced motion. **Alternatives** playful variant for younger users; clinical variant when paired with providers. **Differentiate** personal insight over raw data; a gentle, recognizable voice.

## Fitness and sports

**Goals** start, track, and review sessions; compare performance. **Density** glanceable during activity, rich afterward. **Trust** accurate data sources and units. **Navigation** prominent start action, activity history, plans. **Visual** high contrast, bold numerals, energetic accents, dark mode for outdoor and evening use. **Emotion** motivating, competitive where the audience wants it. **Failures** small controls in motion, unreadable outdoors, buried pause. **Access** large tabular numerals, haptic and audio cues. **Alternatives** coach-led, community, or quantified-self emphasis. **Differentiate** session feel (haptics, sound, live metrics) and meaningful post-session stories.

## Banking and finance

**Goals** check balance, pay, transfer, understand spending. **Density** medium. **Trust** paramount: clear amounts, recipient confirmation, fees, security status, recoverable errors. **Navigation** accounts home, pay/transfer as primary action, activity, support. **Visual** restrained color, strong hierarchy for amounts, tabular figures, few accents. **Emotion** calm, competent. **Failures** ambiguous transaction states, hidden fees, alarming error copy, decorative charts. **Access** screen-reader friendly amounts and dates, no timeouts without warning. **Alternatives** youth or neobank tone with more expression; private banking with premium restraint. **Differentiate** clarity of money movement and proactive, plain-language help.

## Trading and investing

**Goals** monitor positions, analyze, place orders quickly and correctly. **Density** high for active traders, medium for long-term investors. **Trust** real-time status, data delay notices, order review, risk disclosures. **Navigation** watchlist, portfolio, trade entry, orders; fast symbol search. **Visual** dark and light themes, compact rows, tabular decimal-aligned numbers, locale-aware up/down color plus sign and arrow, minimal decoration. **Emotion** precise and steady, not casino-like. **Failures** confetti and gamified urgency, jittering numbers, ambiguous order states, hidden fees, color-only change. **Access** compact layouts that still reflow at large text; summaries for charts. **Alternatives** beginner mode with progressive disclosure versus pro mode with density. **Differentiate** speed and certainty of order entry, information design of charts and positions.

## Enterprise productivity

**Goals** finish work tasks fast across many records. **Density** medium to high. **Trust** permissions clarity, audit trails, sync status. **Navigation** role-based home, search-first, lists to detail. **Visual** neutral, systematic, information-first, consistent tables and forms. **Emotion** efficient, dependable. **Failures** desktop screens squeezed onto phones, offline loss, permission errors after input. **Access** keyboard and switch support on tablets, large text in data views. **Alternatives** field-work variant with large targets and offline-first. **Differentiate** fewer steps for daily tasks, smart defaults, excellent offline behavior.

## Consumer productivity

**Goals** capture and organize quickly. **Density** low to medium, adjustable. **Trust** sync reliability, undo. **Navigation** quick capture always reachable, lists and views, search. **Visual** clean, personal, light delight in micro-interactions. **Emotion** clear-headed, lightweight. **Failures** feature sprawl, slow capture, configuration before value. **Access** dictation-friendly input, clear focus order. **Alternatives** minimalist versus power-user views. **Differentiate** capture speed and a distinctive, calm organizing model.

## E-commerce

**Goals** discover, evaluate, buy, track. **Density** medium; image-led. **Trust** price clarity, delivery dates, returns, reviews, secure checkout. **Navigation** home/discover, search, cart, orders, account. **Visual** product imagery first, consistent crops, restrained chrome, clear price hierarchy. **Emotion** confident, aspirational for premium brands. **Failures** dark patterns (false urgency, sneaked items), surprise costs, slow image loads, tiny variant selectors. **Access** product image descriptions, accessible variant pickers. **Alternatives** premium editorial versus value-oriented dense catalog. **Differentiate** product storytelling, fit or sizing confidence, and frictionless reorder.

## Education

**Goals** learn in short sessions, track progress, practice. **Density** low to medium. **Trust** accurate content, child safety for young audiences. **Navigation** continue learning first, path or curriculum, practice, profile. **Visual** clear content hierarchy, friendly illustration, readable long text. **Emotion** encouraging, curious; playful for children, respectful for adults. **Failures** gamification overwhelming learning, small text in lessons, guilt mechanics. **Access** captions, transcripts, dyslexia-friendly spacing, adjustable pace. **Alternatives** academic, corporate training, or child-focused variants. **Differentiate** feedback quality on mistakes and a visible sense of progress.

## Social networking

**Goals** consume, create, connect. **Density** media-dense feed. **Trust** safety controls, privacy, reporting, blocking. **Navigation** feed, discover, create, notifications, profile. **Visual** content-first chrome, recognizable creation action, consistent media treatment. **Emotion** expressive, belonging. **Failures** infinite engagement loops without controls, hidden privacy settings, unmoderated harmful content surfaces. **Access** alt text prompts, captions, reduced autoplay. **Alternatives** close-friends versus public broadcast models. **Differentiate** creation tools and healthier controls over the feed.

## Messaging

**Goals** send and receive reliably, find conversations. **Density** medium. **Trust** delivery state, encryption cues where real, block/report. **Navigation** chat list, conversation, calls or contacts. **Visual** clear sender distinction, legible bubbles, timestamps on demand. **Emotion** personal, immediate. **Failures** ambiguous delivery states, composer under keyboard, lost drafts. **Access** screen-reader message grouping, voice message transcripts. **Alternatives** workplace messaging with threads and density. **Differentiate** reliability cues, search, and expressive but legible conversation design.

## Travel

**Goals** search, compare, book, manage trips offline. **Density** medium to high in search results. **Trust** total price, cancellation terms, time zones, reliable offline itinerary. **Navigation** search, trips, saved, account. **Visual** inspiring imagery in discovery, utilitarian clarity in itineraries. **Emotion** anticipation, reassurance during disruption. **Failures** hidden fees, time zone confusion, itinerary unavailable offline. **Access** clear dates and times with zone, large boarding information. **Alternatives** business-travel efficiency versus leisure inspiration. **Differentiate** disruption handling and an itinerary that works offline at the gate.

## Food delivery

**Goals** choose, order, track arrival. **Density** medium, image-led menus. **Trust** price and fee breakdown, accurate ETA, substitution handling. **Navigation** discover, search, cart, order tracking. **Visual** appetizing photography, clear prices, prominent tracking state. **Emotion** easy, satisfying. **Failures** surprise fees, unclear order status, tiny customization controls. **Access** allergen information as text, accessible customization. **Alternatives** grocery variant with lists and substitutions. **Differentiate** honest ETAs, easy reorder, and graceful substitution.

## Healthcare

**Goals** book care, view records and results, message clinicians, manage medication. **Density** medium. **Trust** clinical accuracy, privacy, clear sources, emergency guidance. **Navigation** appointments, records, messages, medications. **Visual** calm, high-legibility, conservative color, strong status semantics. **Emotion** reassuring, respectful. **Failures** jargon, alarming result presentation without context, hidden urgent contact options. **Access** older-adult friendly sizes, plain language, caregiver access. **Alternatives** clinician-facing variant with higher density. **Differentiate** result explanations in plain language and smooth handoff to care.

## Media and entertainment

**Goals** browse, play, continue. **Density** image-dense rows. **Trust** clear subscription and download state. **Navigation** home, search, library/downloads; persistent mini player. **Visual** dark immersive surfaces, artwork-led, minimal chrome during playback. **Emotion** immersive. **Failures** autoplay with sound, lost playback position, unreadable text on artwork. **Access** captions, audio description, accessible player controls. **Alternatives** kids profile with simplified navigation. **Differentiate** discovery that respects taste, and seamless continue-watching across devices.

## Family applications

**Goals** coordinate schedules, share, keep children safe. **Density** low to medium. **Trust** roles and permissions visible, child privacy, location sharing consent. **Navigation** family home, calendar, members, settings by role. **Visual** warm, friendly, soft geometry, distinct member colors with names. **Emotion** reassuring, inclusive. **Failures** surveillance tone, unclear who sees what, complex setup. **Access** works for grandparents and children, large targets, plain language. **Alternatives** premium calm wellness tone versus playful children's tone. **Differentiate** shared rituals and roles that make coordination feel light.

## Creator and AI applications

**Goals** create, iterate, export. **Density** medium; tool-dense editors. **Trust** clear AI provenance, editable outputs, usage limits, data use disclosure. **Navigation** projects, create, library. **Visual** canvas-first, quiet chrome, clear generation states. **Emotion** empowering, playful when appropriate. **Failures** opaque progress, lost work, overpromising capability, unlabeled AI content. **Access** non-gesture alternatives for editing, readable generated text. **Alternatives** pro tools with dense controls versus guided templates. **Differentiate** controllable generation, version history, and trustworthy provenance.

## Gaming

**Goals** play, progress, socialize. **Density** varies by genre; menus must stay scannable. **Trust** transparent purchases and odds, parental controls. **Navigation** play first, then progression, store, social. **Visual** distinctive art direction; HUD legibility over spectacle. **Emotion** fun, tension, reward. **Failures** manipulative monetization, unreadable HUD text, inaccessible controls. **Access** remappable controls, subtitles, color-blind modes, reduced flashing. **Alternatives** casual versus competitive interface density. **Differentiate** art direction and menus that feel part of the game world.

## Operations and logistics

**Goals** complete tasks in the field: scan, confirm, route, report issues. **Density** medium to high, task-focused. **Trust** sync and offline status, proof of delivery, error recovery. **Navigation** task queue, current task, map/route, issues. **Visual** high contrast for outdoor use, large targets for gloves, status-driven color with labels. **Emotion** efficient, dependable. **Failures** small targets, no offline mode, data loss on interruption. **Access** one-handed operation, haptic confirmation, voice input. **Alternatives** dispatcher tablet view with dense tables. **Differentiate** scan-to-done speed and certainty under poor connectivity.
