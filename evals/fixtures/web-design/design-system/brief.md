# Cargoline: product brief (accepted, v1.2, 2026-10-20)

Product: web application for tracking B2B freight shipments across road and rail in the Nordics.

Users
- Dispatchers at logistics companies: desktop, 8-hour shifts, manage 200–2,000 active shipments, act on exceptions.
- Customer contacts at shippers: occasional visits, often on phones, check status and download documents.

Accepted experience requirements
- UX-1 Dispatchers see every open exception without scrolling the default view at 1440×900.
- UX-2 Shipment status is understandable without color (text label and icon).
- UX-3 Target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- UX-4 A customer can read a shipment's status and ETA on a 360px-wide screen.
- UX-5 Dispatchers can switch between compact and comfortable table density.

Brand: logo wordmark in green #0B6E4F; no other brand assets. Tone: calm, precise, no hype.
First release: shipment list, shipment detail, exceptions queue, customer status page, sign-in.
Out of scope for v1: billing, carrier management, mobile apps.
Open decision: whether customers get their own login or a signed link (affects sign-in design).
