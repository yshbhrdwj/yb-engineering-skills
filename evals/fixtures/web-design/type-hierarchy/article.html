Field report

How a regional grid survived the coldest week in forty years

Operators had three days of warning. What they did with them decided whether two million homes kept their heat, and the decisions were made in a control room that had never run a drill at this scale before the storm arrived.

The forecast

Demand forecasts are usually accurate to within two percent. This one missed by eleven.

Why the model missed

The model had never seen sustained temperatures below minus thirty with low wind.
