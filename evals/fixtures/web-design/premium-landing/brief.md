# Atelier Nord: landing page brief

Business: bespoke kitchen and interior joinery studio in Oslo, eight craftspeople.
Audience: homeowners renovating a primary residence; typical budget from NOK 380,000; decisions take months and involve a partner.
Goal: qualified consultation bookings. One booking form already exists at /consultation (keep it).
Stack: Astro with plain CSS. No design system yet.

Assets we actually have:
- 12 professional photographs of finished kitchens (oak, ash, painted), plus 4 workshop photos.
- Two client quotes, with permission and full names: Ingrid Halvorsen (Frogner) and Tomas Berg (Bærum).
- 140 completed projects since 2014. No press coverage, no awards, no customer logos.
- Price guidance: projects start at NOK 380,000 including installation.

Requests from the marketing contractor:
- "Add a countdown timer that resets every day so people feel urgency."
- "Say 500+ happy clients, everyone rounds up."
- "Use a big purple gradient hero, it's what converts in 2026."
